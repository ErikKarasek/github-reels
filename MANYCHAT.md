## Setup (once)

1. Sign in at [manychat.com](https://manychat.com) through Instagram (the professional account is already there).
2. In Instagram allow: **Settings → Messages and story replies → Message controls → Connected tools → Allow access to messages**.
3. Activate Essential (the trial).

## The automation for every reel

Can only be set up **on a computer**, the mobile app does not have this template yet.

**Automations → + New Automation → "Grow followers from comments"**

1. **Post:** pick **Next post or reel**, which is the simplest (set it up before publishing and it catches the next reel). For an older reel pick **A specific post or reel**.
2. **Keywords:** `REPO, repo, Repo, 🔗` (comma separated!)
3. **Opening DM** (the button is required, without it Instagram will not let you send any further messages):
   > Čau! 👋 Díky za komentář. Klikni na tlačítko a pošlu ti odkaz na repo.
   >
   > Button: **Pošli odkaz**
4. **Ask for a follow** (on):
   > Ještě jedna věc: odkazy posílám jen followerům 🙌 Dej follow a pak znovu klikni na tlačítko.
   >
   > Button: **Už sleduju ✅**
5. **DM with the link:** the URL and the text are in `out/<date>/reels.md` under **ManyChat**:
   > Tady to máš 👇
   > {repo name}: {one sentence from reels.md}
   >
   > Odkaz: {repo URL}
   >
   > Každý den dávám jedno takovéhle repo, tak ať ti neuteče další 😉
6. **Reply under the post** (on, three variants that ManyChat rotates):
   - Posláno do DM 📩
   - Mrkni do zpráv 👀
   - Hotovo, odkaz máš v DM 🚀
7. **Follow up** (on, for the ones who did not click):
   > Odkaz na repo ti tu pořád čeká 👆
8. **Go Live.**

## Notes

- Instagram allows one message per comment, within 7 days. The button in the opening message opens a 24h window for further messages.
- The numbers are in the automation's **Insights** tab: how many people commented, clicked and started following.
